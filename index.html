A Letter For You

My dearest Afshal,

Happy Birthday to the most beautiful person who has found her way into my heart. ❤️ Today is your day, but somehow I feel like I am the lucky one, because I get to celebrate the fact that you exist and that our paths crossed.

I don't know if words can ever completely explain what you mean to me. Sometimes it is the smallest things about you that stay in my mind — your smile, the way you talk, the little things you say, and the moments when you make me smile without even trying.

You have become a beautiful part of my life, and I genuinely hope this new year of your life brings you everything your heart quietly wishes for. I hope you laugh more, worry less, achieve the things you dream about, and always remember how special you are.

If I could give you one gift beyond anything wrapped in a box, it would be the ability to see yourself through my eyes for just one moment. Maybe then you would understand why your smile means so much to me and why having you in my life feels like something worth being grateful for.

On your birthday, I don't just wish you happiness for today. I wish you countless beautiful mornings, peaceful nights, exciting adventures, unforgettable memories, and a life filled with people who truly value your heart.

Thank you for being you. Thank you for the memories, the conversations, the laughs, and even the little moments that may seem ordinary but somehow become special because they are connected to you.

May this birthday be the beginning of one of the most beautiful chapters of your life. Keep shining, keep smiling, and never forget that you deserve every beautiful thing life has to offer.

Happy Birthday once again, my beautiful Afshal. ❤️

Forever yours,
Ali 💗
